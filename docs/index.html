      Records with coordinates and no geospatial issue, read live from the GBIF API. The density layer shows every record. Points you can click: all records up to 3,000; above that, up to 1,200 records of the current view.
      Normalized current: above 1, climate concentrates the current in that cell relative to a uniform landscape; below 1, it diverts it. Each layer is the median over the temperature (BIO1 to BIO11) or the precipitation variables (BIO12 to BIO19) in each cell. Zone conductance: for each tile, the median over BIO1 to BIO11 of its conductance relative to uniform resistance, averaged over the tiles that cover the cell, each weighted most at its centre; low is a barrier, near 1 is open. Transverse connectivity: current from west to east between lowland cells (warmer than the climate at 2000 m at that latitude), for lowland species. Longitudinal connectivity: current from north to south between montane cells, for montane species. Main configuration: WorldClim 2.1 (1970 to 2000), margin q = 0.1, tiles of 200 km. A pass is robust when its median stays on the same side of 1 in five configurations of margin and tile size. Connectivity covers 11°N to 23.5°S; the census covers the whole Andes. Data: github.com/irwingss/andean-passes.
